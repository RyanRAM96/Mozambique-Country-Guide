República de Moçambique · South-east Africa

MozambiqueTerra da Boa Gente · Land of good people

A long country on the Indian Ocean, shaped by a thousand years of trade, almost five centuries of Portuguese presence, a war for independence and a civil war after it. This guide covers its history, its land and wildlife, and the peoples and languages that make it up.

Five turning points

Five dates that shaped Mozambique

  1. 1498

    Vasco da Gama reaches Ilha de Moçambique on his way to India, beginning nearly five centuries of Portuguese presence.

  2. 1895

    Portugal captures Ngungunhane, emperor of Gaza, and completes its conquest of the south.

  3. 1964

    FRELIMO launches the armed struggle for independence with an attack at Chai, in Cabo Delgado.

  4. 1975

    Independence on 25 June, with Samora Machel as the first president.

  5. 1992

    The Rome peace accords end a 15-year civil war that cost about a million lives.

National symbol

Reading the flag

Adopted in 1983, the flag grew out of FRELIMO's own banner. It is one of very few national flags to show a modern firearm. Proposals to remove the rifle have been debated, most visibly in 2005, but it remains.

  • Green · the riches of the land
  • White · peace and the justice of the struggle
  • Black · the African continent
  • Yellow · the country's mineral wealth
  • Red · centuries of resistance to colonialism and the armed struggle for independence
  • Star · international solidarity
  • Book · education
  • Hoe and rifle · farming and production, and the defence of the nation
